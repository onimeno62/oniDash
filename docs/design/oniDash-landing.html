Built for a real library

Everything important. Nothing pretending to be important.

oniDash focuses on the work a personal media library actually needs: discovering what you own, keeping it organized, and making it pleasant to use every day.

⌕

Search across everything

One shared search layer for your collection, with full-text indexing and ranked results instead of isolated catalogue silos.

◈

Metadata without lock-in

Extract metadata automatically, edit it manually, and keep catalogue information useful even when files move or drives disappear temporarily.

↯

Safe filesystem indexing

Recursive scanning, identity-based upserts, missing-file handling and background jobs are designed for large local collections.

♫

Music that feels like music

Artists, albums, tracks, playlists, favourites, history, queue management, artwork and a proper playback workspace.

▣

Purpose-built media views

Movies, manga and books do not have to behave like music. Each catalogue gets its own useful experience while sharing the same foundation.

⌘

Local by design

Your catalogue and database live with you. oniDash is built around local folders, local storage and a local service on your machine.

The experience

A shared foundation. A different rhythm for every medium.

The shell stays consistent so you never have to relearn the application. The content adapts to what you are actually doing.

Music workspace

More than a folder full of audio files.

Turn a filesystem collection into a proper music library. Browse artists and albums, manage playlists and favourites, edit metadata, inspect files, build a queue and resume playback.

Project principles

Quiet software for a noisy collection.

Local-first

The catalogue is designed to live on your machine, alongside the files you already own.

Non-destructive

Indexing observes your filesystem. It does not need to take ownership of your media.

Specialized by design

Music, movies, manga and books share infrastructure without being forced into one generic UI.

Automation with control

Scanning and metadata workflows can be automated while remaining visible, editable and recoverable.

Open foundation

The project is developed in the open, with a documented architecture and a conventional web stack.

Windows practical

Built around local folders, multiple drives, a local service and the realities of a personal Windows machine.

Build your library
